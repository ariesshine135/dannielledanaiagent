This book isn't another rehash of design thinking theory. It's the book I wish I'd had when navigating my first founding designer role in an AI-native startup.

Honest about what's hard. Doesn't pretend there's a formula. But gives you frameworks, stories, and tools that actually work.

About the Author

Dannielle Dan

Dannielle is a product design leader with 10+ years building AI-powered B2B products across cybersecurity, fintech, ed-tech, and customer experience. She's led design at companies like PayPal, Microsoft, Armorblox, and Medallia.

Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, she's a team builder, mentor, and advocate for design's role in shaping the future of AI-powered products.
